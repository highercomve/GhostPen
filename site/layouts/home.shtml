Three keys from better text

No copying into a chat window and back. GhostPen works where you are writing.

Select the text

In your mail, editor, browser, chat: any app where you can select text.

Press Ctrl Shift A

The menu opens next to your work with the selection loaded. Or click the tray icon.

Pick an action

Proofread, make it professional, casual, concise, expand it or translate it. The result replaces your selection.

Everything for the text you write

One small app in your tray, ready in every program.

Rewrite in place

Proofread, professional, casual, concise, expand and translate, at three intensities. Add your own actions, or type any instruction in the prompt bar.

Built-in models

GhostPen runs the model itself with its embedded llama.cpp, on the GPU (Vulkan, CUDA, Metal) or the CPU. No Ollama or server needed, nothing leaves your machine.

Or any AI endpoint

Ollama, LM Studio, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq or any OpenAI-compatible API. Keep several profiles and switch between them.

Live captions

Subtitles for whatever your computer plays: meetings, videos, podcasts. Transcribed on your machine with whisper, optionally translated.

Voice dictation

Press Ctrl Shift D and speak. The transcript is polished by your AI profile and waits on the clipboard.

Text from images

Copy a screenshot and GhostPen extracts its text with a vision model, ready to edit or translate.

Built-in models

Your own AI, run by GhostPen itself

Download a model from the settings and GhostPen runs it with the llama.cpp compiled into it. No server to install, no account, nothing sent over the network.

  • A curated catalog: Qwen3.5 and Gemma, from 1.3 GB to 6 GB.
  • Downloads pause, resume, and are checked against Hugging Face's checksums.
  • Models you already have in LM Studio are found and reused.
  • On the GPU: Vulkan or CUDA on Linux, Metal on macOS. As much of the model as the free GPU memory holds; the rest on the CPU.

Small and native

Built with Oriel, a desktop framework in Zig: a native window, the system webview, and the AI engines compiled in.
