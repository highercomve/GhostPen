Your computer does the work

Download the model.
Keep the data.

GhostPen can run its own AI model on your GPU or CPU. Once the model is downloaded, built-in editing works offline. Your selected text, clipboard images, and transcribed audio are processed on your machine.

  1. 01
    Download a modelOpen Settings → Built-in models and choose one.
  2. 02
    Use a Built-in profileThe model runs inside GhostPen, on your device.
  3. 03
    Keep writing, even offlineSelect text in any app. The result comes right back.

You can also choose an external AI provider. With an external profile, your requests go to that provider. Choose Built-in to keep processing on your machine.

See GhostPen at work.

From rough drafts to live captions, these everyday moments can happen on your computer.

Rewrite in place

Proofread, change the tone, or shorten a sentence. The result replaces your selection where you’re writing.

Translate in any app

Translate the selected text and keep your conversation going without switching windows.

Your own instructions

Add custom actions or tell GhostPen exactly what you want it to do.

Beyond the keyboard

Speak, listen, read aloud, and read images.

Speak. Polish. Copy.

Dictate, then polish the transcript using your built-in AI profile. The result is ready on your clipboard.

Follow every conversation

Live captions for meetings, videos, and podcasts, transcribed on your computer with Whisper.

Hear it read aloud

Select text, or a result, and GhostPen reads it in a natural voice that runs on your computer, in the language of the text.

Turn images into words

Copy an image, extract its text with a built-in vision model, then edit or translate it.
